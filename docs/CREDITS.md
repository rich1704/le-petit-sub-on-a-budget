# Credits

- **Le Petit:** Jean Hiraga's design, published in *L'Audiophile* no. 29 (1983). The sub is built to
  sit next to it — see [Le Petit on a budget](https://github.com/rich1704/le-petit-on-a-budget).
- **Driver data:** SB Acoustics, datasheet *10″ SB26SFCL38-4, Preliminary data, Rev. 1 (16.09.2022)*.
- **3D model:** [three.js](https://threejs.org/).

Thank you to everyone who shares plans, measurements and listening notes — this project stands on
that work.
